## 1. What this project is

**Feasibility-First Whole-Body Navigation on SONIC.** Pipeline: RGB-D + goal + proprioception →
scene-route-motion feasibility model F(o, r, m) → body-aware route/behaviour planner → motion
tokens → **frozen** SONIC tracker → Isaac Lab → sim2sim → real G1. Full plan:
`docs/guidance/2026-08-19_feasibility_first_navigation.md`.

The one non-negotiable design rule: **SONIC stays frozen in the main experiment.** The project's
strongest asset is causal attribution — with the controller frozen, outcome differences are
attributable to the *choice* (route/motion), not to training drift. Joint end-to-end training is
explicitly rejected. Three training lines are kept separate: Stage A (SONIC fine-tuning on
deployable skills — orthogonal, not the main claim), Stage B (counterfactual feasibility
pretraining on SweepCF, pairwise-rank loss), Stage C (closed-loop BC → DAgger → small PPO).

Gate roadmap: **G0** pre-registered decorated/random/SweepCF selector comparison → **G1** real
RGB-D+proprio+token data pipeline → **G2** frozen-SONIC single-obstacle closed loop in Isaac Lab →
**G3** deployable motion-bank fine-tuning → **G4** sequential traversal → **G5** route
counterfactuals / body-aware navigation → **G6** sim2sim, then real G1 (arm-tuck regime first).

### 4.2 P9 — void attempt, driver hardening, and a completed-but-unadjudicated rerun

**P9** (`docs/prediction_register.md`): retiming the adapted clip pays back the transport cost.
Three predictions — (1) adapted cells pass the tracking gate (endpoint < 0.35 m); (2) the
correction lands where the arithmetic says (`adapted_easy` within 0.06 m of nominal's 0.217 m);
(3) the 2×2 survives (nominal_hard strikes, adapted_hard clears).

**The void attempt** (recorded in the register, uncommitted): the first rollout used a retimed clip
converted with `--scene-start 0 0 0` while its causal family used `-2.0 0 0`, so the crouch met the
ceiling 60 frames early, still upright. It produced a clean-looking, well-formed, completely
meaningless 2×2. Consequence: `run_deployable_family.preflight_route` now refuses to spend a
rollout when a deployable clip's `scene_start_xyz` differs from its causal family's. The driver
also gained: `CONTENDED_REQUIRED_MIB = 9000` (GPU-contention margin above the 6000 a rollout
needs), `ROLLOUT_TIMEOUT_S = 1800` (Isaac hangs rather than exits on failed scene creation), and
`INFRASTRUCTURE_MARKERS` so an infra failure is never scored as a rejected cell.

**The corrected rerun COMPLETED at 17:56 on 2026-08-19** and nobody has adjudicated it yet.
Results in `/data/robotixx/groot-wbc-kimodo-m0/wsA/deployable_family/n_013_ceiling_overhead_left/family_scores.json`:

| cell | accepted | endpoint (m) | peak non-foot (N) | reasons |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| nominal_easy | yes | 0.217 | 45 | — |
| adapted_easy | **no** | **0.565** | 15 | `unstable_reference_drift` |
| nominal_hard | no | 1.308 | **1544** | `disallowed_robot_contact`, drift |
| adapted_hard | **no** | **0.565** | 15 | `unstable_reference_drift` |

On its face: **prediction 1 is refuted** (0.565 > 0.35; retiming recovered only ~0.08 m of the
0.42 m shortfall, so the linear payback model is wrong), prediction 2 falls with it, and the
*geometric* half of prediction 3 held (nominal_hard strikes at 1544 N, adapted_hard clears at
15 N, no contact). A new rejection reason appeared: the retimed clip trips the
`unstable_reference_drift` gate (`gate_policy.py:218`) that the un-retimed clip did not.

**Before writing the register entry, verify the rerun is valid** — this project just paid for that
lesson once:
1. Confirm the retimed clip used for this run was converted at `--scene-start -2.0 0 0` (check
   the clip's manifest under `wsA/deployable/` and that `preflight_route` ran; the score pattern —
   no early strike, hard-cell contact on the *nominal* only — is consistent with correct placement,
   but consistent is not confirmed).
2. `adapted_easy` and `adapted_hard` report **bit-identical** endpoint and force (0.564661941…,
   15.23766975…). Plausible iff the crouch touches nothing in either scene and the sim is
   deterministic — verify the two trajectory pickles genuinely differ in scene but agree in
   trajectory, rather than one rollout having been scored twice.
3. Check `drift_rate_mps` in the diagnostics against `max_drift_rate_mps` — the retimed clip runs
   slower by construction; make sure the drift gate is not mechanically punishing slowness itself
   (if it is, that is a finding about the gate, and it goes in the register as such, not as a
   silent re-grade).

Then write the P9 adjudication into `docs/prediction_register.md` in the register's house style:
what was predicted, what was measured, which predictions fell, what the honest mechanism reading

## 6. Rules of the road (violating any of these voids results)

- SONIC stays frozen in the main experiment. `sonic_release/last.pt` is the checkpoint.
- Causal-reference artifacts (matched duration/root path — what the paper's 2×2 is reported from)
  and deployable-skill artifacts (retimed — for fine-tuning/hardware) are **separate families of
  artifacts**. Never mix them in one comparison; never amend the pre-registration because a
  deployable clip changed.
- Success is the explicit `SONIC_EVAL_SUCCESS` marker + `success_manifest.json`, never exit
  status ("process exit status: 0 (not authoritative)" is printed for a reason).
- Register predictions before running; record void attempts rather than discarding them;
  infrastructure failures are unevaluable, not rejected.
- The physics gate is the verdict; geometric proxies explain, they never re-grade.
- Scene instances ≠ families. Five shelf heights on one nominal is one family.
