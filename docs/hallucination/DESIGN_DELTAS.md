# LFH Design Deltas

**Status:** Accepted at the Phase-0 review gate on 2026-08-20, subject to the
conditions recorded in `REVIEW_PHASE0.md`. These are reconciliations with
repository evidence, not changes to LFH's scientific invariants. Physics
remains the verdict, test scenes remain frozen, and every generated family
retains one changed causal constraint.

| ID | Design assumption | Repository evidence | Adopted adjustment |
|---|---|---|---|
| D0-001 | LFH code can be placed in one script-oriented directory | Reusable geometry, gating, authoring, and scoring already live in `gear_sonic/dataset_generation/`; command entry points live in `scripts/research/` | Put reusable LFH modules under `gear_sonic/dataset_generation/hallucination/`, thin commands under `scripts/research/hallucination/`, and tests under `tests/dataset_generation/`. |
| D0-002 | Ten fixed-radius semantic keypoints faithfully screen constraints | The authoritative model is 29 capsules on 14 links; feet and torso are multi-capsule and there is no head collision link | Define semantic capsule groups over all existing owners. Tier 1 reports grouped semantic extrema; Tier 2 evaluates the full capsule set. Do not invent keypoint radii. |
| D0-003 | The verified set contains multiple duck families | `duck_002` and `duck_003` share the `005`/`056` nominal/adapted source and current indexing conflates variant and family identities | Use `cf_005_056` as one causal source with distinct variant IDs. Keep `mf_005_c08` as the other independent historical family. Variants do not advance the 24–30-family gate. |
| D0-004 | Both golden sources provide paired executed empty-room delivery | `cf_005_056` has nominal/adapted plane probes; the local `mf_005_c08` adapted rollout found in modebank ran in an easy shelf scene | Refuse executed-window synthesis from `mf_005_c08` until an approved plane reroll exists. Never substitute a commanded reference or a shelf-scene rollout. |
| D0-005 | Current episode columns support full DCS | The 35-column flat index lacks constraint axis, coordinate, stable source identity, variant identity, and binding group; `motions.csv` lacks joined trackability. Prior audit also records a missing `mf_005_c08`, a void family re-admitted without a route check, and a progress-board count of three | Make an additive index-contract repair the first Phase-1 task. DCS reports explicit unknown bins and independent-family counts; exactly two independent families is the acceptance test. |
| D0-006 | Existing face sizes establish non-skirtable constraints | Shelf and wall extents are fixed constants; the route check only proves root-footprint intersection | Derive face extent from both swept bodies over the critical interval plus margin, then verify non-binding keepout and route intersection. Reject rather than enlarge in a way that introduces a second cause. |
| D0-007 | Baseline shelf friction/restitution are known numeric values | Generated USDA and scene-USD terrain configuration do not attach an explicit obstacle material | Preserve the baseline omission/runtime default. Record material provenance as unknown/default; do not assert numeric values. |
| D0-008 | Physics contact records identify the contacted scene primitive | Payloads retain robot external-force signals but not obstacle-prim identity; the capsule proxy loses practical resolution around 10 micrometres and may lead force contact by roughly 14 frames | Use forces only to establish physical contact. Attribute it offline to authored primitive AABBs using capsule distance, preserving the measured ambiguity floor and reporting timing disagreement without tuning. Ambiguous or non-unique attribution is an `unattributed` refusal. Instrumentation would require a later reviewed delta. |
| D0-009 | Overhead, lateral, and floor edit operators are available | Only `local_crouch` and `local_arm_tuck` modify trajectories. Side-step, narrow-pass, and step-over logic are predicates | Limit v1 to crouch/overhead and anatomy-compatible arm-tuck/lateral cases. Defer floor/support and stance/step edits to v2. |
| D0-010 | All four requested camera views are existing rollout outputs | Ego is recorded; room/side views are CPU reconstructions; chase and wrist require separate simulation passes; the current overhead pass is blank | Build v1 evidence sheets from ego plus CPU room/side renders. Budget additional simulated views only through a later explicit GPU manifest. |
| D0-011 | A measured shelf read-back generalizes to every constraint | Current read-back targets `LowShelf_00` by name | Author a stable constraint handle and read its emitted world AABB generically. Require requested-versus-measured binding coordinate error of at most 0.5 mm. |
| D0-012 | A 50 mm margin already enforces single-cause keepout | Fifty millimetres appears as an easy-scene offset, but no `C \\ B` swept-volume validator exists | Use 50 mm only as the configurable initial keepout/easy margin. Measure non-binding capsule clearance explicitly for nominal and adapted trajectories. |
| D0-013 | Scene counts and golden-family counts in prose are authoritative | The tree contains 157 USD-family files; release CSV, JSONL, and manifest views disagree on verified-family identity/count | Compute inventory from artifacts and publish reconciliation warnings. Never convert asset/variant counts into independent causal evidence. |
| D0-014 | Dataset-generation tests are already in CI | Tests exist under `tests/dataset_generation`, while current GitHub Actions cover docs only | Add focused local tests with Phase 2. Treat new dataset CI as separately reviewed repository-maintenance scope. |
| D1-001 | Adding the missing route check is sufficient to exclude the void matched-family cells | The loaded shelves for `mf_x002_c08` and `mf_x003_c08` still intersect the route because their footprint is broad, but their centers are exactly 2.0 m from the manifest binding station | Require both route intersection and a manifest-to-loaded-constraint station offset of at most 0.5 mm for source verification. Retain larger-offset variants as explicit `not_verified` rows. |
| D2-001 | One crossing-frame interval can represent both motions in a source family | The `duck_003` nominal and adapted empty-room executions reach the same shelf station at different frames (approximately 125 and 95) | Store an inclusive crossing interval per motion in `ConstraintSpec`. Both intervals describe the same measured station and binding face; no temporal alignment is invented. |
| D2-002 | The `mf_005_c08` nominal plane execution exists, so one adapted reroll completes its source evidence | Exhaustive matching of exact `w_nominal*.pkl` and `w_crouch08*.pkl` runtime logs finds no `scene=plane` execution. The modebank pair ran in `cf_005_056_easy`; local runs used shelf scenes. The legacy manifest also omits axis/motion identities and contact attribution. | Require two approved plane probes and a later evidence-preserving metadata/attribution repair. Until all gaps close, `extract_spec` reports every missing prerequisite and E1b for this family remains refused. This supersedes the narrower D0-004 reading. |
| D2-003 | Golden `shelf_plank` should copy the historical shelf's complete 0.5 m × 3.0 m footprint | At the binding station, the union of both executed capsule spans plus the required 50 mm margin needs 1.0811 m along the route and 3.0 m across it. A 0.5 m route depth does not certify anti-skirt coverage. | Preserve the fixed golden underside coordinates, static Cube collision, 3.0 m width, and runtime-default material, but derive the route depth from executed evidence. The CPU golden reproduces the cause coordinate, not a known-insufficient footprint. |
| D2-004 | Pair-local names such as `easy.usda` and `hard.usda` are sufficient generator outputs | The rollout driver discovers scene IDs only as direct `assets/scenes/<package>/<scene_id>.usda` files; nested pair directories and repeated generic names are not addressable. | Emit deterministic package-level names `spec__archetype__seed__cell.usda` plus a pair-specific manifest. The tuple `(spec, archetype, seed)` remains the reproducing key. |
| D2-005 | Preventing an overhead route skirt requires the binding face to contain the complete capsule union along and across the route at one crossing frame | Recomputing reach over the generated 1.0811 m plank includes the adapted motion after its local crouch releases: adapted-hard capsule clearance becomes −38.91 mm, reversing the intended CPU sign. The shipped 0.5 m × 3.0 m footprint has +66.66 mm adapted-hard capsule clearance and produced the verified physics pattern. A finite face is a temporal intervention; along-route depth determines its active interval. | Preserve the source's measured 0.5 m along-route footprint for the golden and derive anti-skirt coverage only across the route. Require both root routes to cross the face, recompute semantic reach over the exact finite footprint, and refuse any generated pair whose four capsule-clearance signs do not match the target. This supersedes D2-003's along-route enlargement while retaining its 3.0 m across-route coverage. |
| D2-006 | The runner's documented bare `plane` condition can adjudicate controller trackability | A successful 199-frame, 50 Hz V3 capture omitted `left_foot_ground_contact_force_w`, `right_foot_ground_contact_force_w`, and `support_floor_prim_path`; the acceptance gate therefore correctly returned unevaluable. This reproduces the earlier failure documented in `docs/tuck_trackability_is_not_predictable.md`. | Retain bare-plane captures only as non-verdict swept-volume evidence. Trackability probes use the existing `screen_empty` scene, whose real registered floor enables pair-resolved support evidence and whose walls/high shelf are remote. Require zero external collision contact so remote geometry cannot substitute for emptiness. V4 supersedes V3 before a scientific probe verdict exists. |
| D2-007 | The scalar 0.70 chest-delivery prior is conservative enough to admit top-ranked arm-tuck scene proposals | Both arm-tuck pairs were trackable with zero external contact, but position-aligned KCS delivery was only 4.32/56.58 mm (0.076) on the left and 17.41/58.00 mm (0.300) on the right, versus 42.00/41.62 mm predicted. Both executed windows are below the 20 mm scene-spend floor. | Refuse both scene proposals despite successful trackability. Append the 20 per-keypoint response rows, keep D_phi refused at one amplitude per side, and use stronger/multi-level existing-operator calibration before proposing lateral geometry. A trackable edit is not necessarily a delivered edit. |
| D2-008 | E2 can use the default two-source denominator after empty-room probes | `mf_005_c08` adapted failed the reference endpoint gate, leaving only `cf_005_056` extractable; treating variants as a second source would violate D0-003. | Run the preregistered three-archetype transfer on the one eligible source (12 cells), report the denominator explicitly, and refuse any multi-source generalization. Preserve the 2/3 variant threshold as a context-transfer test, not an independent-family claim. |
| D2-009 | Multi-level calibration will make at least one current arm-tuck proposal scene-eligible | All 12 calibration captures tracked with zero external contact, yet the corrected matched-level wrist fits predict only 6.66/10.84 mm delivery near a 60 mm command; conservative lower bounds are 1.10/4.76 mm. One left response even widens by 3.65 mm at the smallest command. | Retire the scalar delivery prior and refuse current E3 lateral scenes. Keep the fitted D_phi models as proposal-only evidence. A later cohort must strengthen the existing operator and clear a 20 mm conservative executed-delivery bound before any lateral geometry spend. |
| D2-010 | Response scale alpha is bounded to the first edited command in [0, 1] | CAL1's alpha=1 anchor is a 60 mm target. The existing operator's strict-CPU-gated range continues to roughly 100-117 mm; renormalizing stronger commands back to 1 would merge distinct interventions and corrupt matched-level fitting. | Keep alpha=1 fixed to the CAL1 60 mm anchor and permit any finite nonnegative scale. Strong commands use alpha>1 (for example 2.0 for a 120 mm target). Group model replicates by exact registered alpha and retain actual per-keypoint `commanded_mm` as the interpolation coordinate. |
| D2-011 | The strength screen's centre/half-width proxy is the commanded per-keypoint response used by LFH | For `092/right`, the centre proxy reports 95.37 mm while the authoritative KCS capsule-surface wrist command is 264.51 mm. They answer different questions; equating them would silently change the delivery fit. | Mark the centre proxy `selection_proxy_only`. KCS per-keypoint capsule surfaces remain authoritative for D_phi and face proposals. Report proxy/KCS disagreement without turning it into an acceptance gate. |
| D2-012 | A one-sided root-relative reach can be compared directly with a lateral-gap coordinate | `ConstraintSpec` and the archetypes define the coordinate as the full separation of two world-fixed faces. The old proposal path compared that gap to a root-relative half-width, ignored route drift and the opposite face, fixed alpha at 1, and left lateral `face_across_route_m` unused. | Add exact `lateral_gap_reach`: twice the largest capsule support about the fixed gap centre, over both faces and the finite route-height rectangle. Keep one-sided reach diagnostic-only, allow the registered `max_alpha`, parse open coverage buckets, extend binding panels outward by the declared anti-skirt extent, and require critical binders inside the time-mapped edit interval. |
| D2-013 | Hashing a `screen_empty` trajectory is enough to promote it into a reusable `ConstraintSpec` | `screen_empty` is gradeable only because its registered floor supplies support evidence, and its remote geometry is empty only when the accepted rollout records zero external contact. The v0.1 schema previously allowed only bare `plane`, blocking the valid CAL2 evidence while also lacking a way to prove the stronger condition. | Permit `screen_empty` only with a hash-pinned run-record/cell adjudication. `validate_pair` rechecks completed+accepted, zero external force, and exact trajectory path/hash before loading the motion. Bare-plane golden evidence remains backward compatible. |
| D2-014 | Clearing the scalar 20 mm CAL2 gate directly yields a lateral scene | The 24-trial full-gap search retained only one temporally active, same-side result. `084/right` has a 165.80 mm raw window and a 56.41 mm noise-certified intersection with target rank 48; `092/right` is refused because its apparent windows bind outside the edit interval or on the opposite anatomy. | Instantiate only the `084/right` 0.30 m pinch-panel candidate at the `wrist_right × 0.8_0.9 × clear_25_50` target. Preserve the 23/24 CPU attrition, keep `092/right` refused, and require the four-cell physics pilot before calling this a family. |
| D2-015 | The empty-room capsule certificate plus the E1a uncertainty margin remains sufficient after a lateral face changes the controller trajectory | The E3 pilot matched 3/4 outcomes, but adapted-hard contacted its binding panel at frame 115 before drift at frame 142 and rejected. The accepted adapted-easy execution at the same station and 0.30 m face extent requires a 0.906489 m full gap, already 6.49 gap-mm above the registered bin's 0.900 m upper edge; its uncertainty-certified minimum is 0.942577 m. | Refuse the rank-48 target and do not spend a near-boundary retry. Treat empty-room windows as proposal gates only. After scene-conditioned evidence exists, recertify from accepted, zero-external-contact trajectories; never use a contact-contaminated trajectory as clearance evidence. A wider retry must target a different DCS bucket and receive a new registration. |
| D2-016 | Every empty admissible DCS bin is reachable by some current motion/operator pair | CAL3 accepted 3/4 new crouch pairs and found exact, temporally active head/torso windows up to 72.26 mm, but all 36 finite-face trials yielded zero target matches. Their certified coordinates fall in the already occupied 1.2–1.3 m head/torso bucket; other head/torso buckets do not intersect, while shoulder targets violate the proposer's binding-anatomy contract for a non-skirtable overhead face. | Separate **empty** from **currently feasible** coverage targets. Preserve `coverage_target_empty` as a successful funnel refusal, do not instantiate an already occupied bin under E3's coverage objective, and audit target templates against operator-keypoint coupling before the next coverage release. Keep CAL3 executions as operator evidence rather than manufacturing a scene claim. |
| D2-017 | Any indexed episode may fill a DCS causal target, and the configured target cross-product is feasible | V1 counted calibration `probe` rows and cells from refused sibling variants as occupancy. Three probe rows alone suppressed `head_torso × overhead × 1.2_1.3 × clear_25_50`; 48/168 targets also paired `local_crouch` with shoulder binders that `propose.COUPLING` forbids. Replaying CAL3 after removing those errors restores 20 proposals across all three accepted pairs. | Supersede D2-016's zero-candidate conclusion with SweepCF-DCS v2. Only canonical cells from an exact verified variant occupy causal targets; the coupling-valid denominator is 120, not 168. DCS becomes a reporting/novelty term after trajectory-feasible support, never the sole proposal gate. Historical v1 remains reproducible from its config. |
| D2-018 | E1a's 18.044 mm maximum observed range is a calibrated uncertainty bound, and D_phi supplies validated predictive intervals | E1a has three values per cell over eight overhead cells and no cross-operator confidence statement. `delivery.py` uses an in-sample residual q90; leave-one-motion-out interval coverage promised by the method has not been run. | Call 18.044 mm an **empirical engineering margin**, not a confidence bound. Apply it symmetrically to clear/strike sides in E6. Treat D_phi output as screening evidence until held-out-motion calibration is possible; exact accepted executions remain authoritative. |
| D2-019 | Maximizing empty absolute DCS bins is the learning-optimal scene objective | E3 spent on an empty but context-infeasible lateral bin while initially discarding three feasible independent crouch sources. DCS omits route phase and finite face exposure, both of which changed window existence in the fixed search. E5 also lacked equal-budget placement baselines. | Distinguish unknown natural `P_env`, deterministic trajectory-feasible `P_feas`, and designed proposal distribution `q_LFH`. Parameterize support by source, operator, axis, keypoint, route progress, finite extents, paired margins, and normalized window position. Evaluate learning value against uniform-feasible, DCS-ranked, and visual-only controls on held-out source × archetype splits. Physics remains the label source. |
| D2-020 | The widest engineering window is the best representative atom for each source | E6 verified sources 089/090 but source 086's widest atom, with a 0.30 m route-aligned face, failed adapted-easy trackability without contact. E6c held source, motions, operator, station, archetype, target, and margin rule fixed; reducing exposure to 0.10 m changed adapted-easy to accepted and produced a complete, uniquely attributed 2x2 family. | Retain the full continuous support census and treat widest-per-source as an audit baseline, not the proposal policy. Include finite exposure in `q_LFH`, preserve the verified 0.10 m source-086 atom during E7, and require staged easy/context recertification before every hard transfer. E6 remains 2/3; E6c is separate adaptive evidence. |
| D2-021 | Matching the four target outcome labels is sufficient to verify archetype transfer | All 24 E7 labels matched and every nominal-hard contact was uniquely binding, but source 086's door-lintel contact occurred at frame 122 after reference drift at frame 95. | Require binding contact before drift in addition to label agreement and unique attribution. Keep the door-lintel as a causal refusal; E7 remains 5/6 even though its raw label score is 6/6. |
| D2-022 | Four sources with three verified archetypes each are sufficient for a held-out source × archetype learner test | E7/E7c satisfy that count, but only `shelf_plank` and `ibeam` are common across all four sources; `hanging_panel` exists only for 086 and `door_lintel` is refused there. A joint holdout would still confound source and archetype. | Replace the count-only E5 gate with a complete crossed matrix of at least four sources × three common verified archetypes. Run E8 hanging-panel transfers for `cf_005_056`, 089, and 090 before E5. |

## Explicit Non-Deltas

- No learned scene generator or learned verdict model is introduced.
- No frozen evaluation scene is materialized, inspected, or changed.
- No user-owned prediction-register entry or claim-5 artifact is touched; autonomous LFH entries
  are filed and reconciled under `GOVERNANCE.md`.
- No reference-only motion is relabeled as executed evidence.
- This document does not itself authorize GPU work; immutable manifests are self-approved under
  `GOVERNANCE.md` before spend.
