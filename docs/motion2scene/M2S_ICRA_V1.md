## Predictions before this study's acquisition

1. Analytic and Motion2Scene each yield at least one walk-fail/d040-pass group per carrier.
   This is the guidance's hypothesis, not our established expectation: the preceding
   pilot accepted 1/6 analytic and 0/6 learned slots. Report the prediction even if it fails.
2. Uniform yields at most one useful contrast across its 18 requested generated groups.
3. Target-only construction yields both-pass on at least 12/18 requested generated groups
   (the guidance's two-thirds fraction, with its obsolete 9-group denominator corrected).
4. The achieved-transition target screen has zero false-clears among actually executed
   target-clear predictions. Report both contact-qualified failures and tracker failures;
   the screen remains a proxy forecast, never an outcome label.
5. Each contrast arm improves traversal over uniform by at least three net discordant
   conditions. Motion2Scene–analytic is two-sided, without a directional prediction.

Interpretation A: M2S >= analytic and both outperform uniform/target-only; B: analytic
>= M2S with contrast benefit; C: no demonstrated selector benefit. Equality of point
estimates is neither equivalence nor noninferiority. Unequal acquired counts preclude
an equal-complete-label interpretation; retain an equal-requested-slot study instead.
No funding, venue submission, or acceptability decision is inferred from these outcomes.

walk/d040 executions supply achieved geometry. New paired labels use 8722; these are
repeated development conditions, not fresh physics seeds or source-held-out transfer.

only the geometric evaluator uses achieved entry/passage/exit clouds. The existing

backgrounds (two per carrier). Background allocation is fixed: 41001 absent/raised;
41002 absent/blocked; 41003 raised/blocked. Thus six backgrounds globally, each type
twice, shared identically across arms. At most 72 generated pairs + six background
pairs = **156 unique command executions**, not 180. The guidance's 180 count gives
six backgrounds per carrier and conflicts with its 18+6 per-arm quota. There are
no replacement proposals. Refusals are completed acquisition outcomes, not labels.

Generate 16 outputs per arm/carrier, assign the first six before independent audit.
Proposal RNG seed 8841 for each source/arm; all outputs and searches are charged.

17-query pattern search in its original trust boxes. Uniform samples the same
[0.1,0.9] station × [1.1,1.45] m height domain. Target-only search sees only d040.

## Learner, evaluation and analysis contract

Use P3's deterministic two-head logistic control: physical scale 1 except joint
velocity 5, zero initialization, Adam 0.01, 2000 full-batch updates, BCE + 0.01 mean
squared weights. Two >=0.5 heads; walk preference; neither-feasible means refusal
followed by walking, not stopping. Train on all complete admitted assigned groups;
report actual count and composition per arm. No optimizer seed sweep. Six leave-four-
assignment-out diagnostic refits per arm use sorted requested-group IDs in consecutive
blocks of four; missing labels remain missing. They measure fitting sensitivity,
not new independent datasets or deployed policies.

The twelve pre-existing station×height layouts remain fixed and source-route-bound:
station .35/.45/.55/.65 × underside 1.18/1.27/1.36, physics seeds 8511/8512. Four learned
controls plus scripted rays and a privileged achieved-geometry analytic selector make
432 traversal assignments. Privileged means it knows beam pose and the recorded empty
transition; it chooses d040 only when the nominal analytic forecast rejects walk and
admits d040. It is not optimal switching-time control. The script uses the same legal
0.30 s request and upper/lower packet rule. Additional absent/raised/blocked outcomes
must remain a separate suite, never mixed into the traversal headline.

Primary endpoint: carrier-averaged passage; paired 2×2 counts by carrier/layout/seed
for M2S–analytic, M2S–uniform, analytic–uniform and M2S–target-only. Show each carrier
and paired differences. Layout/seed repeats are dependent within carriers. Exact
binomial discordance intervals/tests may be descriptive only, with their independence
assumption explicit; they do not establish population inference from three carriers.
Report unnecessary crouching, command-bank inadequacy, successful-walk refusals,
forces, falls, resets and returns separately. The inspected layout slice is development
for this revision; unexecuted combinations are reserved-layout evaluation, not a newly
untouched confirmatory source test. No outcome-table lookup counts as a rollout.
