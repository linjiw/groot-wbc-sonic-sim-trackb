This panel uses the newly generated, physically qualified six-second neutral from development source 41002, SHA256 `ef33c2139f7a6278d670c012edc9db70671d08b6842e8757d15dc6a6b61b3990`. It contains 180 source frames at 30 Hz and 299 loaded reference frames at 50 Hz. This establishes the neutral carrier's horizon; it does not qualify longer adaptations or course transitions.

The [V2 lock](TRAVERSAL_EVALUATION_LOCK_V2.json), its 36 materialized layouts and its original 72 nominal episodes remain unchanged and incomplete. V3 does not reinterpret V2 geometry using a longer reference, replace that panel, or establish source-ancestry transfer.

Python `random.Random(202609081820)` draws every parameter once in the recorded order, without rejection. Four single layouts each cover short, sustained and early passages. Three courses contain two short constraints and three contain a short then sustained constraint. Length ranges are 0.10–0.25 m or 0.50–0.90 m; underside, width, thickness, lateral and yaw ranges retain the prior a priori bounds. All layouts are retained regardless of future feasibility.

Each nominal box and all eight finite stress variants have **fixed world center, yaw and full dimensions** in the JSON. Positions were computed once from the named source's XY arc length and net heading. Future reference changes must not move those boxes. The 36 nominal and 288 stress episodes per policy are separate denominators; physics seeds are repeats of a layout, not independent scene designs. Evaluation layouts, variants, labels and teacher queries are excluded from development and training.

Execution remains pending a separate common implementation lock and an explicit finite-horizon terminal/censoring policy. The inherited V2 contact, completion, upright-hold, fall/reset and reporting conditions remain recorded; its 8 s and 12 s timeouts are explicitly **not adopted** for this finite horizon. Incomplete passage, stabilization and recovery at the endpoint must be defined before evaluation. No wraparound or post-reset observation may extend a trajectory. Course evaluation also requires actual qualification of the full command sequence and horizon. The JSON authorizes geometry reservation only.
