# Finite transition-support diagnostic v1

Post hoc development diagnosis of the frozen construction pilot, not replacement
proposals or physics labels. Keep its twelve assignments and all refusals unchanged.

For all three qualified carriers, use the same seed-8721 empty-transition geometry
and authored reference route. Evaluate a fixed 20 × 71 grid: stations 0.12 to 0.88
in steps of 0.04 and heights 1.10 to 1.45 m in steps of 0.005. Count nominal 10 mm
walk-interference/target-clearance witnesses, then witnesses at the original 17
search offsets. Apply the full 113-offset independent audit to search-passing
centres only; absence of sampled witnesses is not proof of infeasibility. Report
visibility, reserved-neighborhood and pre-decision exclusions separately.

Report the maximum nominal and search-set contrast slack (minimum of target clearance
minus 10 mm and minus walk clearance minus 10 mm). Retain each centre and all computed
arrays, including negative slack. Also measure how many nominal witnesses lie inside
any original learned draw's unchanged station/height trust box (±0.05, ±0.03); this
checks search reachability on this finite grid, not optimizer convergence.

Use only recorded development data. No fitting, threshold adjustment, slot refill or
new simulator run. CPU ceiling 300 seconds. Do not describe nominal witnesses as
uncertainty-robust or physically successful. The purpose is to decide whether the next
separately registered construction version needs better proposals, a different clearly
scoped geometric requirement, or a different supported transition. A nominal task
study may report perturbation failures separately, but cannot inherit the old robust
certificate claim by silently dropping its offsets.
