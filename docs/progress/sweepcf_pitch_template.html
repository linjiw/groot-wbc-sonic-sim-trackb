Same journey. Two rooms. Physics decides.

A dataset that teaches a robot why a motion had to change — not just that rooms look different.

Every episode is a matched pair: one journey, one obstacle, two motions. The simulator says which one survives. Nobody labels it.

The gap we are closing

Scene variation is not scene-conditioned behaviour.

Generate a thousand rooms and a policy learns that rooms vary. It does not learn which part of its body the room binds, or what to do about it.

What everyone has

Scene diversity. A corpus of traversals through different geometry, each one labelled with whether it succeeded. The supervision says succeeded here, never succeeded because it ducked.

What is missing

The contrast. A second episode, identical in every respect except the one edit the geometry forced — so the difference in outcome has exactly one cause, and a learner can attribute it.

Why it is a label you can trust

The verdict is a contact force, not an opinion.

In one of our banded families the unedited walk drives its torso into the ceiling. The edited one clears the same ceiling. Nobody adjudicated that — the simulator reported it in newtons.

A result we did not go looking for

Arms are free. Legs are charged.

Running the corpus through the frozen controller measured something about the controller itself, useful whether or not you believe our dataset thesis.

Ask the robot to pull its arms in and the controller delivers nearly all of it, and walks better while doing so — endpoint error falls from 0.257 m to 0.084 m as the tuck grows. Ask it to crouch and it delivers about half, and pays for every centimetre.

The measurement that changed our pipeline

Commanded is not delivered.

We asked for the smallest edit that clears each obstacle. Then we measured what actually arrived at the surface the obstacle binds against — and it was a third to two thirds of it.

ObstacleBinds onAsked forArrivedRatio
Ceilingtorso90.2 mm39.0 mm43%
Wall, chestupper arm23.8 mm16.7 mm70%
Wall, waistwrist51.4 mm15.5 mm30%

A minimum computed against an optimistic model is optimistic by the same factor, so the rule reliably asked for an edit too small to work. Dividing by the measured ratio is what it always meant. On the same nominal the usable window goes from 23.8 → 75.2 mm at chest and 51.4 → 103.3 mm at waist.

What it cost, and what it bought

Every wasted rollout was predictable on a laptop.

Our last scale-up spent 21 GPU rollouts and produced zero usable families. We stopped it, diagnosed four separate defects — and then checked whether any of them needed a GPU to find. None did.

A batch now launches only from a signed manifest. Routes that leave their room, edits that exceed what the controller will spend, windows that vanish after delivery — all rejected before anything is scheduled.
