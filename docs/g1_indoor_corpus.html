Pipeline

How a text prompt becomes a validated episode

SOURCEKimodo referenceA text-prompted kinematic G1 clip, exported to a 36-column MuJoCo qpos CSV at 30 Hz.
PLACEScene generationThe room and its furniture are built around the motion's swept corridor, so the episode is traversable by construction.
EXECUTESONIC under physicsThe released checkpoint tracks the reference in Isaac PhysX at 50 Hz. Nothing is replayed kinematically.
GATE11 reason-coded gatesRoute error, falls, contact, foot support — each compared against the commanded motion, not an absolute threshold.
EXPORTLeRobot synthetic_g1Causally shifted, joint-reordered, and bound to a typed provenance chain that cross-checks the runtime capture.

The controller is the released SONIC checkpoint, pinned to its G1 encoder. The recorded action.motion_token is the actual decoder input — measured to sit exactly on the FSQ codebook lattice on all 26 rollouts, so it is byte-comparable with the token_state the C++ deployment runtime consumes.

Clutter density

Density as a measured axis, not an adjective

Because the scene is generated around a known-good corridor, density becomes a dial. The same motion and seed at four settings, each rolled out under physics:

PresetMarginPiecesOccupancyRoute clearanceLateral scene contactVerdict
sparse0.60 m1213%0.986 m0.000 Naccepted
moderate0.30 m2628%0.673 m0.000 Naccepted
dense0.20 m3131%0.463 m0.000 Naccepted
tight0.12 m3334%0.287 m0.000 Naccepted

Why tight works at all. A 0.12 m margin is below the measured p95 path error of 0.22–0.23 m, so it looks like it should collide. It does not, because the clutter is generated against the executed swept volume rather than the reference. The robot reproduces that trajectory deterministically, so tracking error is already baked into the geometry the room was built around; the margin only absorbs simulation nondeterminism.

One honest consequence: at tight the 2D route clearance drops below a nominal 0.45 m body radius, and those scenes declare the measured minimum swept half-width (0.273 m) instead. For them the 2D check is a backstop; the authority is the 3D test and recorded physics.

Geometry

The robot is not a cylinder

Clearance used to be a single 0.45 m cylinder around the root. Recording per-body pose and placing the G1's 29 collision capsules in world frame showed that assumption is wrong in both directions at once:

QuantityMeasuredAgainst the old model
Half-width, arms tucked0.273 mfar inside 0.45 m — corridors were oversized
Half-width, median0.377 minside 0.45 m
Half-width, peak arm swing0.664 moutside 0.45 m on 22 of 249 frames
Swept-volume top1.317 m≥1.4 m band occupied on 0% of frames
Foot capsule surface−0.0008 mrecovers the floor plane from pose alone

So a uniformly narrow corridor would be unsafe, and a uniformly wide one wastes space. The corridor now varies per frame. The 1.317 m ceiling is what licenses cantilevered geometry — shelves whose footprint covers the corridor while the robot passes underneath, which a 2D footprint model cannot express at all.

The bug that only eyes caught

Debug markers were leaking the tracking goal into the camera

Twelve episodes passed every schema, physics and provenance gate. Rendering one and looking at it showed bright yellow spheres in the head camera: Isaac VisualizationMarkers drawing the reference body positions the policy was tracking, on 243 of 249 frames, peaking at 15.1% of the image.

The scene axis is genuinely varied: solids split 101 body-height, 45 overhead and 23 floor-level, and the density ladder walks the same motion through clearances from 0.60 m down to 0.12 m without a single lateral contact.

The motion axis is nearly degenerate, and that is the honest headline. Path length varies by 0.465 m and speed by 0.094 m/s across the whole accepted set. The 64-dimensional latent carries an effective rank near 4. Every accepted episode is the same thing: a ~3.5 m forward walk at ~0.7 m/s.

The cause is upstream, not in the generator. Only two source motions are currently usable while Kimodo generation is blocked, so the scene builder multiplies contexts rather than behaviours. Read this corpus as a validated pipeline with a thin behaviour library, not as a training set — a policy fit to it would learn one gait against many backdrops.
